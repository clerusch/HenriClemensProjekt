\begin{abstract}
In line with Eisert and Preskill's call to ``mind the gaps'' on the road to quantum advantage, we propose a certifiable compilation toolchain that advances the transition from heuristic NISQ workflows to verifiable, hardware-aware pipelines. By extending the ZX-calculus with W-spider reasoning, our project delivers rewrite automation, scalable notation, and circuit extraction routines that make classical error-mitigation insights traceable while preparing for error-corrected and fault-tolerant regimes. The following pages summarise the problem landscape, technical programme, and staged milestones for the joint research effort led by Henri and Clemens.
\end{abstract}
